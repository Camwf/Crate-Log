ⓘ Grading Reference Guide
M (Mint): Unplayed, flawless.
NM (Near Mint): Barely played, 1-2 faint marks.
VG+ (Very Good+): Light scuffs, plays great.
VG (Very Good): Visible scratches, some noise, still plays through.
G (Good): Heavy wear, noise throughout, may skip.
P (Poor): Warped/cracked, skips badly.

Cover — check spine first, then corners, seams, and surface (ring wear, writing, stickers).
